WALK-IN SALES SCRIPT — Agentic MSP
====================================
For: Commission sales reps
Setting: Walk-in to service businesses (roofing, HVAC, landscaping, etc.)
Time: 2 minutes max
Goal: Book audit interview OR walk away. No maybes.

════════════════════════════════════════
THE FRAMEWORK
════════════════════════════════════════

Sell OUTCOMES not features.
They don't care about AI, agents, or automation.
They care about: MORE REVENUE, LESS HEADACHE, ZERO OVERHEAD.

════════════════════════════════════════
THE SCRIPT
════════════════════════════════════════

---

OPENING (10 seconds)
=====================

Walk in confident. Smile. No appointment needed energy.

"Hey, I'm [NAME] with MindVault. I work with local service companies
like yours. I've only got 2 minutes -- can I show you something quick?"

[If they say no]: "Totally understand. Here's my card -- when you're
ready to add $50K+ to your bottom line without hiring anyone, call me."
[LEAVE. Don't push.]

[If yes or sure]: Move to the Hook.

---

THE HOOK (20 seconds)
======================

"Right now you're probably spending hours every week on quotes,
follow-ups, scheduling, chasing leads -- all the stuff that keeps
you busy but doesn't make you money."

[Pause. Let them nod or agree. They ALWAYS agree with this.]

"What if all of that just... handled itself? Your leads get followed
up with instantly. Quotes go out the same day. Schedule stays full.
And you never have to hire another person to make it happen."

"That's what we do."

---

THE PROOF / DEMO (60 seconds)
==============================

Pull out phone or tablet. Show the 2-min demo.

"I'll show you real quick."

[PLAY DEMO -- let it speak for itself]

Key talking points DURING demo:
- "This is what YOUR business looks like when it runs itself."
- "See this? That's a lead coming in and getting a response in
  under 60 seconds. At 11pm on a Sunday."
- "Your customers get white-glove service 24/7. You sleep."
- "And here's the part people love -- these agents learn your
  business. Every week they're finding better ways to close leads,
  tighter follow-up sequences, patterns in what actually converts.
  They basically become your smartest employee that never stops
  improving."

After demo:
"So this handles your follow-ups, your scheduling, your customer
communication -- all the stuff eating your day. But it's not just
automation. It actually gets smarter over time. Your agents study
what's working, find better outcomes, and help you and your team
perform better every single month. Less overhead, more revenue,
and it keeps compounding."

---

THE ASK (20 seconds)
=====================

Two paths. Read the room.

PATH A -- They're interested (eyes lit up, asking questions):
"Here's what I'd suggest. We do a free 30-minute audit where we
look at exactly where your time and money are leaking. No pitch,
no pressure -- we'll show you the numbers. If it doesn't make
sense, we shake hands and part friends."

"When works better for you -- this week or next?"

[BOOK THE AUDIT. Get name, phone, email. Shake hands. LEAVE.]

PATH B -- They're skeptical or guarded:
"I get it, sounds too good. Here's the thing -- first 30 days are
free. You don't pay a dime until you see it working in YOUR
business. All I need is 30 minutes to show you the numbers."

"Can I grab your card and I'll reach out to schedule?"

[Get card. Follow up within 24 hours.]

PATH C -- Not interested:
"Appreciate your time. Here's my card."
[LEAVE CLEAN. Don't burn the bridge.]

---

THE CLOSE OBJECTIONS
=====================

"Sounds expensive."
-> "It's less than a part-time hire and works 24/7. First 30 days free.
   If it doesn't pay for itself, you walk away."

"I need to think about it."
-> "Totally fair. What specifically do you need to think about?
   [Listen. Address it. Then re-ask.]"

"My current setup works fine."
-> "That's great. Most of our clients said the same thing -- until they
   saw what they were leaving on the table. Here's what's different
   about this: our agents actually study your operations and find
   improvements you'd never see yourself. One client found out their
   best leads were coming in after hours and nobody was answering.
   We fixed it in a week. 30 minutes. Free audit. Worst case you get
   some good ideas."

"I don't understand the tech."
-> "You don't need to. That's our job. You just run your business.
   We make the other stuff disappear."

"We've been burned before."
-> "I hear that a lot. That's why we do the free audit first.
   No contract, no commitment, until YOU see the value."



════════════════════════════════════════
REMEMBER
════════════════════════════════════════

1. CONFIDENCE. You're offering them a lifeline, not begging.
2. BREVITY. 2 minutes. Say less, listen more.
3. OUTCOMES. More revenue, less headache, zero overhead.
4. THE ASK. Book the audit or leave. No "maybe."
5. LEAVE CLEAN. Every "no" is practice for the next "yes."
