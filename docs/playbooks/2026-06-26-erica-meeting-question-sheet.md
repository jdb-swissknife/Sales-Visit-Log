# Erica Meeting Question Sheet

Purpose: run a tight discovery session that pulls out Erica's actual coaching method for the pilot.

Meeting target:
- confirm pilot interest
- isolate the highest-value coaching moment
- extract the first version of Erica's coaching logic
- define a tiny pilot group and success criteria

## Opening frame

Use this:

"You already have a coaching style that works. I am not trying to replace your coaching. I want to turn your coaching method into something your reps can feel every day, especially right after visits when deals are either moving forward or cooling off."

## Section 1: confirm the pain

1. Where do your reps need your brain the most right now?
2. What do weaker reps consistently miss after a decent conversation?
3. What kind of note or recap makes you think, they had the lead but lost control?
4. Where do reps usually let momentum die?
5. If I only solved one coaching problem first, which one would matter most?

What you want to learn:
- whether after-visit coaching is truly the best first wedge
- whether objection handling, buyer recognition, or next-step discipline is the sharpest first problem

## Section 2: understand her buyer judgment

6. How do you tell the difference between ready-to-buy and just being polite?
7. What are the earliest signs that a lead is actually hot?
8. What makes you downgrade someone from hot to warm or cool?
9. What signals real intent versus financing friction?
10. What patterns do you see with Atlanta homeowners that out-of-market trainers would miss?

What you want to capture:
- her qualification language
- her hot/warm/cool definitions
- local market-specific judgment

## Section 3: extract objection handling

11. What are the top objections your reps hear every week?
12. Which objections are real roadblocks and which are just reflexes?
13. What should a strong rep say when they hear a spouse objection?
14. What should a strong rep say when they hear a price objection?
15. What do weak reps say that immediately loses control?
16. Which objection do your reps mishandle the most?
17. Are there phrases you never want a rep to use?

What you want to capture:
- objection library
- good language vs bad language
- what the AI should recommend and what it should never recommend

## Section 4: define next-step discipline

18. What does a real next step sound like, word for word?
19. What do reps say when they think they set a next step but really did not?
20. When should a rep lock a callback before leaving the situation?
21. What is too vague to count as a follow-up?
22. When should a rep push for a scheduled next step instead of leaving it open-ended?
23. What timing usually works best for the next contact?

What you want to capture:
- callback rules
- acceptable next steps
- vague next-step patterns to flag

## Section 5: define strong rep behavior

24. What does a strong rep consistently do in the conversation?
25. What tells you a rep controlled the conversation well?
26. What tells you a rep had a shot and let it slip?
27. What do your best reps naturally document that weaker reps leave out?
28. What do you wish every rep wrote down after every visit?

What you want to capture:
- strong rep examples
- weak rep examples
- required note fields

## Section 6: shape the pilot

29. Which 1 to 3 reps would give us the cleanest pilot?
30. Would you rather test with newer reps, average reps, or one strong rep plus one weaker rep?
31. How often would you realistically review AI coaching examples during the pilot?
32. What would make you say this is useful within two weeks?
33. What would make you say this is garbage?
34. If the AI gave advice that was directionally right but not in your voice, would you still want it fixed before rollout?

What you want to leave with:
- pilot reps
- review cadence
- success bar
- failure bar

## Section 7: collect source material

Ask for these next:
- 5 to 10 real visit notes or transcripts
- top objections in her words
- examples of strong rep behavior
- examples of weak rep behavior
- buyer-type definitions
- callback / next-step rules
- any scripts, training notes, or voice notes she already uses for coaching

## Close

Use this:

"Perfect. I am going to turn your coaching method into the first coaching profile, load the first knowledge docs, and bring you back a small live pilot so we can compare the AI advice against how you would coach the exact same situations."

## Your score after the meeting

If you leave without these, the meeting was incomplete:
- one clear pilot use case
- one clear definition of hot vs warm vs cool
- top objections list started
- next-step rules started
- first 1 to 3 pilot reps identified
- agreement on what success looks like
