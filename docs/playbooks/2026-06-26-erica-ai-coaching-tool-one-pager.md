# Sales Leader AI Coaching Tool

For Erica and the Atlanta HVAC team

## What it is

This turns your coaching style into a practical daily tool for reps in the field.

Reps already have conversations, knock doors, handle objections, and leave voice notes after visits. The problem is that your best coaching still depends on you being available in the moment.

This tool captures what happened on a visit, pulls out the important details, and gives coaching shaped by how you would actually coach that rep.

Not generic sales advice.
Your method, your standards, your next-step discipline.

## What it does first

The first version stays narrow on purpose.

After a rep logs a visit or voice note, the system can:
- summarize what happened
- identify interest level and objections
- pull out follow-up items and next steps
- generate one coaching card for the rep
- recommend the best next action while the lead is still fresh

## Why it matters

This helps you:
- give more consistent coaching without repeating yourself all day
- make weaker reps less likely to drift after decent conversations
- help newer reps ramp faster
- make sure good leads get a real next step instead of dying in notes
- turn your coaching judgment into a repeatable team asset

## What it is not

This is not:
- a generic sales training platform
- a replacement for managers
- a big dashboard project
- AI roleplay for the sake of AI

The point is simple:
make your real coaching show up faster and more consistently in the field.

## How the pilot would work

We start with a small live pilot.

- 1 to 3 reps
- one use case first: after-visit coaching
- short testing window
- your feedback shapes the coaching style

Each rep uses the app normally.
The system produces coaching recommendations.
You review whether the advice actually sounds like you and whether you would want a rep to follow it.

## What we would need from you

To make the first version useful, we would turn a small amount of your real coaching into the system.

That includes:
- the top objections your reps hear
- what strong reps do differently
- what weak reps miss
- how you separate ready-to-buy from need-to-buy
- what a real next step sounds like

## What success looks like

The early win is not a flashy dashboard.

The early win is you saying:

"That is basically what I would have told them."

If we get there, we can expand it.
If we do not, we tighten the coaching until it sounds right.

## The goal

I am not trying to build AI that teaches sales in general.
I am trying to build AI that coaches like Erica.
